🌱 Everyday Health Tips

Small healthy habits can support your overall wellbeing.

🥗

Eat a Balanced Diet

Include a variety of foods in your meals.

  • Include vegetables and fruits.
  • Choose whole grains when possible.
  • Include protein-rich foods.
  • Drink enough safe drinking water.
🧼

Maintain Good Hygiene

Good hygiene can help reduce the spread of infections.

  • Wash hands regularly.
  • Wash hands before eating.
  • Keep food preparation areas clean.
  • Use clean drinking water.
💧

Safe Drinking Water

Safe water is important for maintaining good health.

  • Use a reliable source of drinking water.
  • Store drinking water in clean containers.
  • Keep containers covered.
  • Follow local water-safety guidance.
🚶

Stay Physically Active

Regular physical activity can support overall health.

  • Walk or perform suitable physical activities.
  • Avoid sitting for very long periods.
  • Choose activities suitable for your abilities.
  • Take rest when your body needs it.
😴

Get Enough Sleep

Good sleep supports physical and mental wellbeing.

  • Maintain a regular sleep schedule.
  • Keep your sleeping area comfortable.
  • Reduce unnecessary screen use before sleep.
  • Give your body enough time to rest.
🧘

Take Care of Your Wellbeing

Mental and emotional wellbeing are also important parts of overall health.

  • Take regular breaks.
  • Talk to someone you trust when needed.
  • Spend time doing activities you enjoy.
  • Seek professional support when necessary.
🛡️

Prevent Illness

Preventive healthcare can help identify health concerns early.

  • Follow recommended vaccination schedules.
  • Attend appropriate health check-ups.
  • Follow advice from qualified healthcare professionals.
  • Do not ignore persistent or serious symptoms.
👩‍🍼

Maternal & Child Health

Mothers and children may need additional healthcare support.

  • Attend recommended antenatal check-ups.
  • Follow vaccination schedules.
  • Monitor child growth as recommended.
  • Contact healthcare workers when concerns arise.
💊

Use Medicines Safely

Medicines should be used according to appropriate healthcare guidance.

  • Follow the prescribed dose.
  • Do not share prescription medicines.
  • Check medicine labels carefully.
  • Ask a healthcare professional when unsure.

⚠️ Important Health Notice

These health tips are for general educational purposes. They are not a diagnosis or a substitute for professional medical advice. If you have serious, persistent, or worsening symptoms, contact a qualified healthcare professional.

🚨 Medical Emergency?

If someone is experiencing a serious or life-threatening emergency, seek immediate emergency medical assistance.
