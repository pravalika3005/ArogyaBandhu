🌿 About ArogyaBandhu

A digital healthcare support platform designed to connect rural communities with health information, healthcare professionals, ASHA workers, pharmacies, and emergency support.

What is ArogyaBandhu?

Technology designed around community healthcare needs.

ArogyaBandhu combines the ideas of "Arogya", meaning health, and "Bandhu", meaning friend or companion.

The platform is designed as a digital healthcare support system for rural communities. It brings together patients, doctors, ASHA workers, pharmacies, and emergency support workflows in one platform.

ArogyaBandhu aims to make health information and healthcare support easier to access, particularly for communities where distance, connectivity, language, or limited healthcare resources can create challenges.

The platform includes AI-assisted health information, digital health records, appointment support, medicine search, health education, multilingual interfaces, and emergency assistance workflows.

Platform Features

Key capabilities included in the ArogyaBandhu prototype.

🤖 AI Symptom Support Provides general health information and symptom-related guidance.
🎤 Voice Assistant Supports voice-based interaction for easier access to health information.
📋 Digital Health Records Helps organize health information and records.
📅 Appointments Provides a workflow for requesting healthcare consultations.
💊 Medicine Support Allows users to search medicines and prepare medicine requests in the prototype.
🚨 Emergency SOS Provides an emergency workflow with location information and emergency calling options.
👩‍⚕️ ASHA Dashboard Supports community health tracking and follow-up workflows.
💊 Pharmacy Dashboard Provides demonstration inventory and medicine request management.

Our Principles

Accessibility

Keep the interface simple and understandable.

Community First

Consider the needs of patients and healthcare workers in local communities.

Multilingual Support

Support multiple languages to reduce language barriers in healthcare access.

Human-Centered AI

Use AI to assist people while keeping healthcare professionals involved in clinical decisions.

⚠️ Important Healthcare Notice

ArogyaBandhu is currently a prototype and is intended to demonstrate healthcare support workflows. AI-generated information should not be treated as a medical diagnosis. Serious or emergency health concerns should be handled through appropriate qualified healthcare professionals and emergency services.
