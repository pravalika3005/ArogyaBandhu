Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about the ArogyaBandhu prototype.

What is ArogyaBandhu?

ArogyaBandhu is a prototype rural healthcare support platform connecting patients, doctors, ASHA workers, pharmacies, and healthcare resources.

Can the AI diagnose diseases?

No. The AI assistant is intended to provide general health information and support. It should not be treated as a medical diagnosis.

Does the emergency feature dispatch an ambulance?

The current prototype provides emergency calling and location-support workflows. It is not connected to a real ambulance dispatch system.

Is this connected to real pharmacies?

No. The pharmacy and medicine features currently demonstrate the intended workflow using prototype data.

Are health records stored securely?

The current prototype uses browser local storage for demonstration. A production healthcare system would require secure storage and appropriate privacy controls.

Which languages are supported?

The prototype is designed around English, Telugu, and Hindi support, including planned voice interaction.

⚠️ Healthcare Safety Notice

ArogyaBandhu is a demonstration prototype and is not a substitute for professional medical care. For serious or emergency health concerns, contact an appropriate qualified healthcare professional or emergency service.
